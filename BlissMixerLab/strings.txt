BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_TRACK_GUIDANCE
	EN	Last.fm similar-track guidance

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_TRACK_GUIDANCE_DESC
	EN	Controls the bounded influence of Last.fm track similarity among local Bliss candidates. Zero disables similar-track lookups. Default: 25.

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_TRACK_GUIDANCE_UNAVAILABLE
	EN	Requires the LastMix plugin to be installed and enabled.

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_ARTIST_STRATEGY
	EN	How Last.fm artist matches affect candidates

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_ARTIST_STRATEGY_DESC
	EN	Choose how Last.fm artist matches affect candidate selection. Per-candidate boost scores each endorsed candidate individually and uses the Lab setting below. BlissMixer target share instead uses BlissMixer's existing Last.fm artist percentage; the Lab boost setting is disabled in that mode.

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_ARTIST_STRATEGY_INFLUENCE
	EN	Per-candidate boost (Lab mode, recommended)

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_ARTIST_STRATEGY_TARGET
	EN	Target share from BlissMixer's setting

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_ARTIST_INFLUENCE
	EN	Last.fm artist boost strength

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_ARTIST_INFLUENCE_DESC
	EN	Sets the maximum boost for a candidate whose artist is endorsed by Last.fm. Zero disables artist guidance; 25 is about 1.8x, 50 about 3.2x, and 100 up to 10x. This control applies only to Per-candidate boost; BlissMixer target share uses the percentage configured by BlissMixer instead.

BLISSMIXERLAB_LAST_PLAYED_INFLUENCE
	EN	Last-played influence

BLISSMIXERLAB_LAST_PLAYED_INFLUENCE_DESC
	EN	Re-ranks the bounded Bliss candidate pool using Lyrion's last-played timestamp. Negative values favor songs heard longer ago; positive values favor recently played songs. Zero is neutral.

BLISSMIXERLAB_LAST_PLAYED_HORIZON
	EN	Last-played saturation horizon (days)

BLISSMIXERLAB_LAST_PLAYED_HORIZON_DESC
	EN	Controls how quickly last-played differences saturate. After one horizon, the raw time signal is about 36.8% of its starting value; songs played much longer ago become increasingly similar. Default: 180 days.

BLISSMIXERLAB_LIBRARY_AGE_INFLUENCE
	EN	Library-age influence

BLISSMIXERLAB_LIBRARY_AGE_INFLUENCE_DESC
	EN	Re-ranks the bounded Bliss candidate pool using Lyrion's library-added timestamp. Negative values favor older library additions; positive values favor newer additions. Zero is neutral. A full LMS database rebuild can reset this timestamp.

BLISSMIXERLAB_LIBRARY_AGE_HORIZON
	EN	Library-age saturation horizon (days)

BLISSMIXERLAB_LIBRARY_AGE_HORIZON_DESC
	EN	Controls how quickly library-age differences saturate. After one horizon, the raw age signal is about 36.8% of its starting value; very old additions become increasingly similar. Default: 365 days.

	EN	Learned matrix influence

BLISSMIXERLAB_LEARNED_BLEND_DESC
	EN	For multiple adaptive-weighting seeds, controls how strongly the learned matrix influences similarity. 0 is variance only; 100 is learned matrix only. A single seed uses the learned matrix directly.

BLISSMIXERLAB_PLAYCOUNT_INFLUENCE
	EN	Play-count influence

BLISSMIXERLAB_PLAYCOUNT_INFLUENCE_DESC
	EN	Controls how listening history affects track selection. Negative values prefer less-played tracks, 0 ignores play counts, and positive values prefer frequently played tracks.

BLISSMIXERLAB_PLAYCOUNT_INFLUENCE_UNAVAILABLE
	EN	This setting requires Lyrion listening statistics. They are disabled when the server is started with --nostatistics.

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_TRACK_GUIDANCE
	EN	Last.fm similar-track guidance

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_TRACK_GUIDANCE_DESC
	EN	Controls the bounded influence of Last.fm track similarity among local Bliss candidates. Zero disables similar-track lookups. Default: 25.

BLISSMIXERLAB_LASTFM_TRACK_GUIDANCE_UNAVAILABLE
	EN	Requires LastMix and the Adaptive weightings and Last.fm artist weighting options to be enabled in the original Bliss Mixer settings.

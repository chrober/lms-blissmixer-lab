# Third-party notices

Bliss Mixer Lab includes code adapted from Craig Drummond's [Bliss Mixer](https://github.com/CDrummond/lms-blissmixer).
Its packages include separately released [bliss-mixer](https://github.com/chrober/bliss-mixer) and [bliss-learner](https://github.com/chrober/bliss-learner) binaries.
The learner describes itself as a port of [bliss-metric-learning](https://github.com/Polochon-street/bliss-metric-learning), whose upstream repository had no explicit license when reviewed; the scope of reuse and any required permission remain unresolved.
The binaries' individual dependency licenses and notices must be reviewed against their exact build sources and Cargo.lock files. This note is not a complete third-party license inventory.
